1. What Nmap flag did you use to find the specific software versions running on these ports?
Answer: sudo nmap -sV -p- [IP] 

2. Why is it dangerous to run an intense scan (like -A) on a fragile legacy system?
Answer: One analogy I saw is that running an aggressive stress test on a legacy system is like "trying to preform a high intensity stress test on a vintage car with a rusted engine." Legacy hardware often has very limited CPU and RAM. The high volume and speed sent by an intense scan could potentially overwhelm the system's ability to process network traffic which can lead to a functional Denial of Service (DoS). Also old, unpatched services frequently have vulnerabilities. An Nmap prob such as (-A) could trigger this and cause the service to crash.
